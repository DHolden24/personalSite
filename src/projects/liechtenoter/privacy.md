# Liechtenoter Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 21 October 2026_

Liechtenoter is a training log for Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Your data stays on your device. Liechtenoter has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and the developer never receives anything you enter.

## Information the app stores

Everything you enter (events, classes, free-play sessions, tournaments, matches, notes, weapons, references, and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, synced, or shared with the developer or anyone else.

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted along with it, unless you have exported a backup.

## Camera

Liechtenoter asks for camera access only so you can scan QR codes to import items that someone has shared with you. Scanning happens on your device. Camera images are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

You can refuse or revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

On Android, QR scanning uses Google's ML Kit library. Google may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic and usage information about ML Kit's performance. This information does not include your camera images or your training data. See [Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

## Sharing, export, and import

Some features move data out of or into the app, and only when you choose to use them:

- **Share via QR code:** shows a QR code on your screen for another person to scan.
- **Share or backup files:** saves a `.json` or `.csv` file to a location you pick.
- **Import:** reads a file you pick, or a QR code you scan.

Once a file leaves the app, what happens to it depends on where you put it (for example, a cloud drive or a messaging app). That service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Links

When you tap a reference link, it opens in your device's browser or another app. Liechtenoter does not track which links you open.

## Children

Liechtenoter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
