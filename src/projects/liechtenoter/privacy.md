# Liechtenoter Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 24 October 2026_

Liechtenoter is a training log for Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Your data stays on your device. Liechtenoter has no accounts, servers, advertising, or tracking, and the developer never receives anything you enter. The only analytics are the Google ML Kit diagnostics described under [Camera](#camera), which apply to QR scanning on Android only and never include your training data.

## Information the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This includes:

- events (classes, sparring sessions, tournaments, solo training, source study, and others) and their matches and notes
- people you train with or fence against: name, club, weapons, notes, and an optional link
- Fechtbuch techniques, plays, technique groups, and their references
- monthly focus topics, weapons, and settings

Liechtenoter itself never uploads, syncs, or shares any of this with the developer or anyone else.

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted along with it, unless you have exported a backup or it is included in a device backup (see below).

## Device backups and transfers

If your device's own backup or transfer features are turned on, your operating system may include Liechtenoter's data in them. On iOS this includes iCloud Backup, backups to a computer, and moving data to a new iPhone. On Android it includes Google backup and device-to-device transfer. These are controlled by Apple or Google under your device and account settings, and the developer cannot access them. Restoring a device backup may bring the data back after you uninstall.

## Camera

Liechtenoter asks for camera access only so you can scan QR codes to import items that someone has shared with you. Scanning happens on your device. Camera images are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

You can refuse or revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

On Android, QR scanning uses Google's ML Kit library. ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information, such as your device model, operating system version, the app's version, performance metrics, and a per-installation identifier that Google says is not intended to identify you or your device. This information does not include your camera images, the QR codes you scan, or your training data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [ML Kit's data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

On iOS, QR scanning uses Apple's built-in Vision framework. No third-party scanning library is involved, and nothing is sent to Apple or anyone else.

## Photos and location

Liechtenoter never accesses your photo library or your location. The iOS app includes Apple's required permission descriptions for these because components it uses reference those features, but Liechtenoter never requests either permission.

## Sharing, export, and import

Some features move data out of or into the app, and only when you choose to use them:

- **Share via QR code or share file:** shows a QR code on your screen for another person to scan, or saves the same content to a `.json` file. A shared event includes its title, description, and details such as a class's instructor name or a tournament's location. It never includes your matches or your tournament placement.
- **Backup and CSV export:** saves a `.json` backup of all your data, or a `.csv` file of your matches, to a location you pick. Backups include everything listed above, including your notes about other people. CSV exports include your opponents' names.
- **Import:** reads a file you pick, or a QR code you scan.

When you export a file on iOS, a copy of it is also kept in the app's private storage until it is replaced by your next export of the same kind or you uninstall the app. When you import a file, a temporary copy may be kept in the app's private storage until your device clears it.

Once a file leaves the app, what happens to it depends on where you put it (for example, a cloud drive or a messaging app). That service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Links

When you tap a link, such as a technique reference or a link on a person's profile, it opens in your device's browser or another app. Liechtenoter does not track which links you open. The website you visit has its own privacy policy.

## Children

Liechtenoter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
